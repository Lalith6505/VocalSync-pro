Privacy Policy for VocalSync Pro

Effective Date: October 2026

Developer: Lalith Madanayake

Contact: lmadanayake@gmail.com

1. Complete On-Device Processing & Privacy

VocalSync Pro operates 100% locally and offline on your device. The app does not access the internet, has zero network permissions, and connects to no external servers.

2. Media & Storage Access

The application only accesses video and audio files that you explicitly select using the standard Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework). Processed and mastered videos are saved directly to your device storage. None of your media is ever uploaded, copied off-device, or shared.

3. Zero Third-Party Trackers & Zero Ads

VocalSync Pro contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, no telemetry trackers, and no user behavior logging.
